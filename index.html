Our Story

Built around one long table.

Table2Eat started as a six-table pop-up in 2019 — one chef, one market run every morning, one menu that changed with whatever looked best at the stall.

Five years later the market run hasn't changed. We still walk the same three farms every morning before service, and the menu still bends to what they hand us.

What we added is the room: warm oak, low light, and a kitchen you can watch work — because a good meal deserves an audience.
